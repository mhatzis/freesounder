# Spotify brand assets

Official Spotify logo and icon files from Spotify's brand/media kit
(`storage.googleapis.com/pr-newsroom-wp/1/2023/05/Spotify_{Primary,Full}_Logo_RGB_*.png`),
downscaled only (no recolouring, cropping or reshaping).

These are Spotify trademarks, **not** covered by this repository's licence.
They are used solely to attribute Spotify content, as required by the
Spotify Developer Terms and the Design & Branding Guidelines
(https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design):

- Full logo (icon + wordmark) where there is room; icon alone only where
  space is tight or the Spotify brand is already established on screen.
- Green only on pure black/white; on other (tinted) surfaces use the
  monochrome logo: black on light, white on dark. `SpotifyMark` does this.
- Minimum sizes: icon 21 px, full logo 70 px wide. Keep the exclusion zone
  (half the icon height) clear. Never pair it with our own logo.
